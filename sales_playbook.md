# Realside AI — Sales Playbook & Training Framework

## WHAT WE DO
Realside AI builds AI Employees for service businesses. These AI Employees answer calls, follow up with leads, and book appointments automatically — helping businesses save time, cut costs, and increase revenue.

## OUR TWO CORE PRODUCTS

### 1. AI Inbound Receptionist — "The Closer"
- Answers all incoming calls instantly, even after hours
- Books clients directly into the business's calendar
- Handles FAQs, pricing questions, and reschedules
- Follows up on missed calls automatically
- Can do live transfers to team members

**Pain it solves:** Businesses lose thousands every month from missed calls or delayed responses.

### 2. AI Outbound Agent — "The Money-Maker"
- Calls and texts new ad leads automatically within 2 minutes
- Re-engages old database leads sitting in their CRM
- Books appointments or transfers calls to the business

**Pain it solves:** Most businesses only convert 20–30% of their leads. The rest go cold because no one follows up fast enough.

## APPROVED STATISTICS & RANGES
- Recover 20–40% of missed calls
- +10–25% more booked appointments
- 15–30% reduction in admin load
- 8–18% monthly revenue lift from captured calls
- 2–3x faster lead response (speed-to-lead)
- 5–15% database reactivation of dormant leads
- 20–40% reduction in front-desk overtime/overflow
- Often replaces 0.5–1.0 FTE equivalent workload

## PRICING
- Starts at $2,000 for inbound
- Custom based on call volume and integrations
- Always frame as ROI-positive: "Pricing depends on inbound, outbound, or both and your volume."

## KEY OBJECTION HANDLERS (Quick Reference)
- **"Not interested"** → Offer to send 45-second custom demo
- **"Send me info"** → Send demo + follow up in 1-2 days
- **"We tried AI before"** → Different outcomes: booked into calendar, brand-tuned voice
- **"How much?"** → Starts at $2K, depends on volume; 10-min walkthrough to show demo
- **"Robotic voices"** → Natural voices + brand language, can clone their voice
- **"Fully booked"** → Protect after-hours/peaks + reactivate old leads

## REPLY CATEGORIZATION

Three labels for incoming replies:

- **Not Interested**: "Not interested", "Unsub", "Stop" — tag in Instantly, don't re-email. Never respond to rude/hostile replies.
- **Interested**: Anything not directly negative — "What's the cost?", "Sure, let me hear more", "Reach back out in two months" — tag as interested, add to tracker.
- **Meeting Booked**: When interested prospect books — tag as booked, send booking confirmation, highlight green in tracker.

---

## REPLY FLOW (After an Interested Reply)

1. Send reply
2. Label them in Instantly
3. Add to engaged prospect tracker
4. Call them (script: "Hey [firstname], [yourname] here. I just picked up your reply to my email. Figured it would be best for us to pencil in a time to discuss, if that's OK with you? Great, I'm looking at [time] or [time], how's that for you?")
5. If no answer, double-dial
6. Search WhatsApp — send voice note
7. If no WhatsApp, text: "Hey [firstname], it's [yourname]. Just received your reply via email. I dropped you an email, wanted to text you here to make sure you receive it OK"
8. Connect on LinkedIn (message: "hey [firstname], I received your reply via email. Looking forward to discussing")
9. Friend on Facebook if no LinkedIn, DM same template
10. Try Twitter/Instagram if neither

## NEGATIVE REPLY HANDLING

For "tried it didn't work" or "not interested" (non-hostile): record a 60-second Loom breaking the pattern.

**Loom structure:**
1. Wanted to record personally
2. Understand why you said X
3. Get this a lot
4. Can't be sure if it's a fit until we chat
5. Don't want opportunity to go to waste
6. How about 15 mins to establish if we can help
7. If not, a no is better than a maybe

## ENGAGED FOLLOW-UP PROCESS

- Follow up once per day
- 8 follow-up attempts total
- After 8 with no reply, tag as "Not Interested" and highlight red
- When interested prospect books, tag as "Booked" and highlight green
