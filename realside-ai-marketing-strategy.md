# Realside AI — Full Marketing Strategy & Launch Plan

## The Business

**Realside AI** builds and deploys custom AI voice agents for blue-collar service businesses across the US and Canada. We answer their phones, qualify leads, book jobs, follow up on estimates, and handle after-hours calls — 24/7, no staff required.

We handle full setup, integration with their existing tools (ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, etc.), and ongoing management.

---

## The Problem We Solve

Blue-collar service businesses are bleeding money from missed calls:

- **30–40% of inbound calls go unanswered** — that's jobs walking straight to the competition
- If you don't call a lead back within **5 minutes**, your chance of closing drops over **80%**
- Most shops can't afford a full-time receptionist, or the one they have is drowning
- After-hours calls hit voicemail — and the homeowner calls the next guy on Google
- Follow-up on estimates is manual, inconsistent, and falls through the cracks

These owners aren't losing jobs because their work is bad. They're losing jobs because nobody picked up the phone.

## Budget Recommendations

### Starting budget: $3,000–$5,000/month

| Campaign | % of Budget | Monthly Spend (at $4K) |
|----------|-------------|----------------------|
| Cold — Awareness + Pain | 50% | $2,000 |
| Warm — Retargeting | 30% | $1,200 |
| Hot — Conversion | 20% | $800 |

**Bidding:** Start with Lowest Cost (automatic). Move to Cost Cap once you know your target CPL (aim for $25–$50 per demo booked).

**Scaling rules:**
- If a cold ad set holds CPA under target for 3+ days, increase budget 20% every 3 days
- Kill any ad that spends 2x your target CPA with zero conversions
- Rotate new creative every 2–3 weeks to fight fatigue

## A/B Testing Roadmap

Test in this order — one variable at a time:

### Week 1–2: Hook / Creative Format
- Video vs. static image vs. carousel
- Test 3 different opening hooks on video ads
- **Winner criteria:** Highest CTR + lowest CPC

### Week 3–4: Audience
- Interest stack vs. broad targeting vs. lookalike
- Test by vertical (HVAC-only audience vs. all trades)
- **Winner criteria:** Lowest CPL (cost per lead/demo booked)

### Week 5–6: Ad Copy Angle
- Pain-focused (missed calls) vs. outcome-focused (recovered revenue) vs. cost-comparison
- **Winner criteria:** Highest conversion rate on landing page

### Week 7–8: Landing Page
- Long-form vs. short-form
- Video hero vs. text hero
- Calendly embed vs. lead form
- **Winner criteria:** Highest demo booking rate

### Ongoing:
- Refresh creative every 2–3 weeks
- Test new verticals as separate ad sets
- Test new proof points as you collect customer results
