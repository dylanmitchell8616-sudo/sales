# Realside AI — Demo Call Script (15 Minutes)

---

## Pre-Call Prep (2 min before)

- Pull up their website — know their services, service area, team size
- Check their Google reviews (tells you their reputation and volume)
- Have a voice agent demo recording ready for their specific vertical
- Have their CRM integration screen ready (ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro)

---

## Minute 0–2: Open (Build rapport, set the frame)

> "Hey {{first_name}}, appreciate you jumping on. I know you're busy so I'll keep this tight — 15 minutes, tops."

> "Before I show you anything, quick question — what made you book this demo? What's going on with your phones right now that made you curious?"

**LISTEN.** Let them talk. They'll tell you the exact pain point to sell back to them. Common answers:
- "We miss a ton of calls"
- "I need a receptionist but can't justify the cost"
- "After-hours calls go to voicemail"
- "We're slammed during peak season"
- "My office manager is overwhelmed"

Mirror it back:
> "Got it — so the main issue is [their words]. That's exactly what this is built for. Let me show you how it works."

---

## Minute 2–7: The Demo (Show, don't tell)

> "I'm going to play you a real call that our voice agent handled for a {{vertical}} business. This is an actual call — not scripted, not staged."

**Play the recording.** Let it speak for itself. Don't talk over it.

After the recording:
> "That call came in at [time]. No receptionist. No office manager. The voice agent picked up in 1 ring, qualified the lead, confirmed the address, and booked the appointment. The owner got the details on his phone 30 seconds later."

> "Any questions about what you just heard?"

Address the inevitable questions:
- **"Can callers tell it's AI?"** → "Most can't. And honestly — homeowners don't care if it's AI or a person. They care that someone answered and helped them."
- **"What if the call is complicated?"** → "If the voice agent can't handle something, it routes the call to you or your team instantly. It's not replacing you — it's making sure no call gets dropped."
- **"Does it work with my software?"** → Show the integration screen. "We plug directly into [their CRM]. Appointments show up automatically. No double entry."

---

## Minute 7–10: Make It Personal (Connect to their business)

> "So for {{company_name}}, here's how it would work:"

Walk through their specific scenario:
1. "Homeowner calls about [their most common service]. Voice agent picks up, asks what they need, confirms the address is in your service area, and books the appointment."
2. "You get a text and an email with the lead details. The appointment shows up in {{CRM_name}} automatically."
3. "After hours, weekends, holidays — same thing. Every call handled."

> "Let me ask you this — on a busy day, how many calls do you think you're missing?"

(They'll usually say a number. If they don't know, tell them:)
> "Most {{vertical}} companies miss 30–40% of their inbound calls. If your average ticket is ${{X}}, even 3 missed calls a day is ${{math}} per week walking out the door."

Let the math sink in.

---

## Minute 10–12: Social Proof + Results

> "We've got {{vertical}} clients right now seeing [specific result]. One guy in [city] told me he added ${{X}}/month in the first 60 days — all from calls he used to miss. No new marketing. No new hires. Just answering the phone."

> "The businesses that get the most out of this are the ones that are already getting calls but can't keep up. Sounds like that's where you're at."

---

## Minute 12–14: Pricing & Close

> "So let me walk you through what it looks like to get started."

**Pricing framework** (adjust to your actual pricing):
> "Setup is [one-time fee] — that covers building your custom voice agent, training it on your business, integrating with {{CRM_name}}, and testing it until it's perfect."

> "Monthly is [monthly fee] — that covers the voice agent running 24/7, all call handling, ongoing optimization, and support from our team."

> "Most of our clients are ROI-positive in the first 2 weeks. If you're recovering even 2–3 extra jobs per week at your ticket size, the math pays for itself multiple times over."

**Trial close:**
> "Does this make sense for where you're at right now?"

If **YES:**
> "Great. I can get you into onboarding this week. We'll have your voice agent live within 5–7 days. I'll send you the intake form right after this call. Sound good?"

If **MAYBE / need to think about it:**
> "Totally fair. What's the main thing you're weighing? [Address the objection]. How about this — let me send you a recap of everything we covered plus a case study from a {{vertical}} company in a similar spot. Then let's connect again on [specific day] so you can ask any follow-up questions."

If **NO / not now:**
> "No problem at all. When does your busy season start? [They answer]. Alright — the smart move would be to get set up before that hits, so you're not scrambling. I'll follow up in [timeframe] unless you want to move sooner."

---

## Minute 14–15: Wrap & Next Steps

> "Appreciate your time, {{first_name}}. I'll send you [recap/intake form/case study] right after this. Any other questions before we hop off?"

> "Good luck out there. Talk soon."

---

## Objection Handling Cheat Sheet

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "It's too expensive" | "I hear you. Let me ask — how much is a missed $800 job costing you right now? If you miss 3 a day, that's $12K/week. Our monthly fee is a fraction of what you're losing. This isn't an expense, it's a recovery tool." |
| "I need to talk to my partner/wife" | "Makes sense. Want me to send a quick summary you can share with them? And let's put a tentative follow-up on the calendar for [day] so we don't lose momentum." |
| "I want to try it first" | "Totally get it. Here's what I can do — we set it up, and if it doesn't work the way I showed you today, we make it right. You'll hear every call recording and see every booking. Full transparency from day one." |
| "We already have a receptionist" | "That's great — this isn't about replacing her. It's about covering the calls she can't get to. When she's on another line, on lunch, or when calls come in after hours. Think of it as her backup that never clocks out." |
| "What if customers don't like talking to AI?" | "Fair concern. Here's what we see — customers care about getting their problem solved fast. If they call and someone picks up, gets their info, and books them in, they're happy. The alternative is voicemail, and nobody likes leaving voicemails." |
| "I'm not ready right now" | "When does your busy season start? Most of our clients wish they'd started 3 months earlier. If we set you up now, you're ready before the rush hits. When would be the right time to revisit this?" |
| "Can I see more examples?" | "Absolutely. I'll send you a couple of call recordings from {{vertical}} clients so you can hear exactly how it sounds. I'll also include a case study with real numbers." |
| "I don't trust AI" | "I respect that. I'm not asking you to trust AI in general — I'm asking you to listen to a real call and decide if it sounds good enough to answer your phones when nobody else can. That's it." |

## Post-Call Checklist

- [ ] Send recap email within 1 hour (Sequence 3, Day 1)
- [ ] Log the call in CRM with notes on pain points, objections, and next steps
- [ ] If closed: trigger onboarding sequence immediately
- [ ] If not closed: set follow-up task for specific date discussed
- [ ] If no-show: trigger no-show recovery sequence
- [ ] Update lead status in pipeline
