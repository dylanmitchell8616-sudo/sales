# Realside AI — Company Profile

## What We Do
Realside AI builds **AI Employees** for service businesses. These AI Employees answer calls, follow up with leads, and book appointments automatically — helping businesses save time, cut costs, and increase revenue.

## Two Core Products

### 1. AI Inbound Receptionist — "The Closer"
- Answers all incoming calls instantly, even after hours
- Books clients directly into the business's calendar
- Handles FAQs, pricing questions, and reschedules
- Follows up on missed calls automatically
- Can do live transfers to team members
- **Pain it solves:** Businesses lose thousands every month from missed calls or delayed responses

### 2. AI Outbound Agent — "The Money-Maker"
- Calls and texts new ad leads automatically within 2 minutes
- Re-engages old database leads sitting in their CRM
- Books appointments or transfers calls to the business
- **Pain it solves:** Most businesses only convert 20–30% of their leads. The rest go cold because no one follows up fast enough.
