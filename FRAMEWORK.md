# Sales & Client Acquisition Framework
## Based on EasyGrow 2.0 (Charlie Morgan / Imperium Acquisition)

---

## Overview

A complete organic client acquisition system designed to attract, book, and close high-ticket clients without relying on paid ads. The framework uses AI-driven strategies, multi-channel outreach, and structured sales processes.

**Target:** Coaches, consultants, agencies, and service providers scaling to $10K-$18K+/month.

### 1.1 High-Ticket Client Acquisition Foundations

- Build the automated funnel that converts attention into appointments

### 1.2 Offer Creation & Positioning
- Craft premium offers that command high prices
- Define your niche with precision

## Phase 3: Lead Nurturing

## Phase 4: Sales & Closing

### Sales Genesis Framework
- **Goal:** Close at 30-40% conversion rate consistently
- Structured discovery process on calls
- Authority-driven selling strategies
- Psychology-based objection handling
- Plug-and-play sales scripts
- Decisive next steps and closing techniques

### Sales Process Flow:
1. **Discovery** - Understand prospect's situation, pain, and goals
2. **Presentation** - Position your offer as the solution
3. **Objection Handling** - Address concerns using proven frameworks
4. **Close** - Guide to a decision with confidence
5. **Onboarding** - Smooth handoff into delivery

## Implementation Priority

### Stage 1 - Foundation (Week 1-2)
- [ ] Nail your offer and positioning
- [ ] Define your ideal client profile
- [ ] Set up your sales process (Sales Genesis)

### Stage 2 - First Outbound Channel (Week 2-4)
- [ ] Choose ONE outbound OS (Loom OS or Loomless OS recommended to start)
- [ ] Build lead list using Lead Generation OS
- [ ] Launch outreach and iterate on messaging
- [ ] Implement Lead Nurturing OS

### Stage 3 - Add Channels (Month 2-3)
- [ ] Add a second outbound channel (Cold SMS or Trojan OS)
- [ ] Start building inbound presence (pick one: IG, X, or YouTube)
- [ ] Refine Sales Genesis based on call data

### Stage 4 - Scale (Month 3+)
- [ ] Layer AI Sorcery across all channels
- [ ] Automate repetitive workflows
- [ ] Consider building a sales team
- [ ] Add remaining channels as capacity allows
